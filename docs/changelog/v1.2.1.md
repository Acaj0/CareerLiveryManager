# v1.2.1

## Fixed
- Applying a livery with "Use Dynamic Registration" on an activity-supported aircraft (e.g. 737 MAX) could leave the previous/default livery "flickering" back depending on camera angle, and show wrong on the hangar/parking display even though it looked correct during an actual mission. The base (full paint) folder is no longer left loose in the shared vendor folder - it's now nested inside the activity's own package folder, where nothing else can pick it up by mistake.
- 737 MAX: a livery that only repaints the parts you'd notice at a glance (fuselage, wings) could show a patch of bare/default color near the wing root, even on an otherwise complete-looking repaint. The 737 MAX splits its exterior into more separate pieces than most aircraft (including one easy to miss - the wing-root section), and every official livery slot ships all of them; a third-party livery that skips one now automatically gets that piece filled in from the plane's own default look instead of showing unpainted.

## Known limitation (not a bug)
- On complex modular aircraft like the 737 MAX, a livery that only changes the engine (and sometimes the wing "shark fin") while the rest of the plane stays default is very likely a partial/registration-only repaint, not a full one - the fallback mechanism only fills in texture files that are actually missing, so an incomplete third-party livery will render incompletely. Try a livery explicitly labeled as a full repaint, or apply with "Use Dynamic Registration" unchecked to test the base folder on its own.
