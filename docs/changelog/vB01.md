# vB01 (beta)

Experimental build from the `beta` branch, based on v1.3.5. It doesn't update itself.

## Added
- **Aircraft that aren't downloaded can get a livery.** Simple aircraft (one livery for the whole plane) are listed even when they aren't in your Official folder, marked "Not downloaded · beta", with a filter of their own. You need to own the aircraft. The HondaJet worked this way with the livery applied by the app, and the Citation Longitude in a manual test; the others are untested.
- Pictures of downloaded aircraft are remembered on your computer, so they are still there if you remove the aircraft later.

## Not included
- Aircraft with Career activities, and the Citation X, A320neo, A321, LVFR A330-900 and PC-12.
