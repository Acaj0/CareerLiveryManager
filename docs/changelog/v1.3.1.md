# v1.3.1

## Added
- **Community screen.** A new *Community* button in the header opens a page with livery creators to follow (MR Liveries and Kiwi Flight Sim: click the picture or the name to open their page) and recommended apps, starting with the MSFS Livery Toolkit for making your own liveries. A red badge on the button points it out until you open the screen for the first time. The links open in your browser; nothing is downloaded or sent from the app.
