# v1.3.4

## Fixed
- **Cessna Citation X: liveries now show up when you fly.** A Citation X livery comes with a pair of folders for the standard aircraft and another pair for the winglets version, and the winglets folders borrow their paint from the standard ones. The app only installed the pair you picked, so with the winglets version the paint was missing (the livery looked right in the menus but the standard livery appeared in flight), and with the standard version the winglets aircraft got nothing. The app now lists the livery once and installs both versions together. Tested in a Career flight on the standard and winglets versions, with and without the Lite textures. This only affects the Citation X; every other aircraft works as before.
